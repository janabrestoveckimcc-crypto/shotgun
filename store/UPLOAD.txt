SHOTGUN KEYBOARD — Chrome Web Store submission package

Status: prepared, NOT submitted to the store.

1. Open https://chrome.google.com/webstore/devconsole using your publisher Google account.
2. Complete developer registration if required. The account owner must supply real account/contact/trader details and approve any registration fee and agreement.
3. Add new item and upload release/shotgun-keyboard-chrome-1.0.1.zip. The manifest must be at the ZIP root. Do not upload the store asset folder as the extension.
4. Use store/listing.json for the listing, permissions, privacy and reviewer fields. Check the current dashboard definitions before making data-use certifications.
5. Upload store/icon-128.png, store/promo-440x280.jpg and store/screenshot-1280x800.jpg. The screenshot presents the actual popup markup and styles, without browser branding; it is an interface preview, not a photograph of an installed Chrome session.
6. Select free distribution, review the completed listing and submit for Google review. Publication is controlled by Google review and does not happen merely by uploading the ZIP.
